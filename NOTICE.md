Individual source URLs follow `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji/main/2D/svg/emoji_u{codepoint}.svg`; the local filename is `{codepoint}.svg`. ## v0.3 metadata

Emoji names and search keywords use Unicode CLDR English and Chinese annotation data, retrieved 2026-10-11 from https://github.com/unicode-org/cldr-json . The accompanying Unicode license is included as `assets/noto/LICENSE-CLDR.txt`. The category grouping uses https://unicode.org/Public/emoji/latest/emoji-test.txt . Selected illustrations cover eight categories without duplicating every skin-tone or sequence variant.
